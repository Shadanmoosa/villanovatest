PHYSIOTHERAPY

Restore mobility, alleviate persistent pain, and optimize musculoskeletal performance in Dubai. Evidence-Based Rehabilitation & Musculoskeletal Health

At Royal Villanova Cosmetics & One Day Surgery Center Dubai, our Physiotherapy Department integrates clinical diagnostic precision, biomechanical science, and hands-on therapeutic modalities to restore effortless, pain-free movement. Rather than merely masking pain, our medical specialists focus on treating the underlying functional source—whether originating from spinal disc compression, sports trauma, joint dysfunction, or chronic ergonomic fatigue.

Under the direct leadership of Dr. Abas Rahimi (17+ years of clinical excellence), each patient benefits from private, one-on-one evaluations, advanced manual therapy, and personalized rehabilitative protocols tailored to achieve swift, long-lasting recovery.

Spinal Health & Lumbar Recovery

Back Pain Relief & Rehabilitation

Chronic or acute lower back pain can severely restrict your daily vitality and work performance. Our specialized spine program addresses conditions such as lumbar disc herniations, sciatica, facet joint stiffness, and muscular spasms through precise spinal mobilization, non-surgical decompression techniques, and deep lumbar core strengthening.

  • Comprehensive spinal mobility and neurological evaluation
  • Gentle manual decompression and facet joint mobilization
  • Core stabilization protocols to prevent recurrent lower back episodes

Cervical Spine & Tension Relief

Neck Pain & Cervical Care

Prolonged desk work, tech-neck posture, whiplash, and cervical disc compression place excessive strain on the cervical spine, radiating tension into the upper back, shoulders, and arms. We employ targeted cervical mobilization, gentle traction, and myofascial release to decompress pinched nerves, eliminate headaches, and restore fluid neck rotation.

  • Targeted relief for tech-neck, cervical radiculopathy, and stiffness

  • Ergonomic neck re-alignment and posture retraining exercises

Sports Injuries & Athletic Rehab

Our sports rehabilitation protocols cover ACL/meniscus recovery, rotator cuff rehabilitation, ankle sprains, runner's knee, and muscle tears. Biomechanical Alignment & Ergonomics

Posture Correction & Realignment

Sedentary lifestyles, repetitive workplace strain, and poor biomechanics lead to rounded shoulders, forward head carriage, thoracic kyphosis, and persistent muscular fatigue. Utilizing digital posture grid assessments and kinetic muscle retraining, our therapists correct underlying muscular imbalances, release chronically tight muscle groups, and strengthen structural stabilizers.

  • Digital posture and spinal curvature assessment grid analysis
  • Correction of forward head posture, slouching, and pelvic imbalances
  • Customized ergonomic workplace guidelines and daily movement routines

Integrative Myofascial Decompression

Cupping Therapy

Experience the clinical power of medically administered cupping therapy. By generating controlled negative pressure on specialized myofascial trigger points, cupping enhances microvascular circulation, stimulates lymphatic drainage, releases deep fascial adhesions, and delivers immediate relief for stubborn muscular knots, back tension, and chronic fatigue.

  • Medically supervised dry cupping & myofascial trigger point therapy
  • Rapid decompression of tight myofascial bands and knots
  • Enhanced blood flow, toxin elimination, and natural tissue repair
