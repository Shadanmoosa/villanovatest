# Gastric Balloon vs Capsule Balloon in Dubai: Which Is Right for You?

*Patient information blog | Draft for clinical review*

Considering a gastric balloon in Dubai, but unsure whether to choose an endoscopic balloon or a swallowable capsule balloon? The main differences are how the balloon enters and leaves the stomach, how long it stays in place, and the care involved.

A capsule balloon is itself a type of gastric balloon. In this guide, “gastric balloon” refers to the endoscopic option, while “capsule balloon” refers to the swallowable Allurion system. Neither is automatically better for everyone: the choice starts with a medical assessment.

## What is a gastric balloon?

A gastric balloon is a temporary device placed inside the stomach to help you feel full sooner and eat smaller portions. It does not involve removing part of the stomach. Treatment should be combined with dietary changes, activity and ongoing support. [[1]](https://www.mayoclinic.org/tests-procedures/intragastric-balloon/about/pac-20394435)

## How does a capsule balloon work?

The Allurion capsule contains a deflated balloon attached to a thin tube. A healthcare professional supervises placement and filling. Routine placement does not require endoscopy or anaesthesia. After approximately 16 weeks, the balloon is designed to empty and pass through the digestive tract. Occasionally, endoscopic or surgical intervention is necessary. [[2]](https://www.allurion.com/en-ae/allurion-gastric-balloon/how-it-works)

## How does an endoscopic balloon work?

A doctor uses a flexible camera passed through the mouth to place the balloon in the stomach, with sedation or anaesthesia. It is later deflated and removed endoscopically. Treatment duration depends on the device: some balloons are designed for six months, while certain other models permit longer placement, up to 12 months under their applicable instructions. [[3]](https://www.endura.com/us/en/home.html) [[4]](https://www.spatzmedical.com/gastric-balloon/)

Ask which exact model is being offered and when it must be removed. A six-month balloon must not simply be left in for a year. [[3]](https://www.endura.com/us/en/home.html)

## Gastric balloon vs capsule balloon: key differences

| Feature | Endoscopic gastric balloon | Allurion capsule balloon |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Placement | Through the mouth using an endoscope | Swallowed as a capsule under clinical supervision |
| Sedation / anaesthesia | Used for endoscopic procedures | Not needed for routine placement |
| Time in the stomach | Device-specific; commonly 6 months, with some models up to 12 months | Approximately 16 weeks |
| Removal | Planned endoscopic removal | Designed to empty and pass naturally; intervention may be needed |
| Volume adjustment | Only with certain adjustable models | Not adjustable after filling |
| Follow-up | Medical and nutritional support | Medical and nutritional support |

Device features and treatment limits vary by model and local labelling. Confirm the proposed device with your doctor. [[2]](https://www.allurion.com/en-ae/allurion-gastric-balloon/how-it-works) [[3]](https://www.endura.com/us/en/home.html) [[4]](https://www.spatzmedical.com/gastric-balloon/)

## Which option may be better for you?

If avoiding a planned endoscopy is a priority, ask whether you are eligible for a capsule balloon. You still need medical screening, follow-up and access to care if symptoms develop.

If a longer period with a balloon is being considered, discuss an endoscopic model with an appropriate treatment duration. Some models allow volume adjustment through an additional endoscopic procedure; this feature does not apply to every balloon. [[4]](https://www.spatzmedical.com/gastric-balloon/)

Your doctor should assess your BMI, previous stomach or oesophageal surgery, medical history and ability to attend follow-up. Eligibility differs between devices. Your goals and preferences matter, but they cannot replace clinical suitability. [[1]](https://www.mayoclinic.org/tests-procedures/intragastric-balloon/about/pac-20394435) [[2]](https://www.allurion.com/en-ae/allurion-gastric-balloon/how-it-works)

## What results should you expect?

Weight loss varies. A balloon staying in place for longer does not, by itself, establish that it is the better choice for you. Ask for evidence about the proposed device and a realistic treatment goal. Continued dietary and activity support matters after the balloon leaves the stomach because weight regain can occur. [[1]](https://www.mayoclinic.org/tests-procedures/intragastric-balloon/about/pac-20394435)

## What side effects should you expect?

Nausea, vomiting and abdominal discomfort can occur after balloon placement. Some patients cannot tolerate treatment and need early removal. Your team should explain the initial diet, prescribed medicines and how to contact them if symptoms persist. [[2]](https://www.allurion.com/en-ae/allurion-gastric-balloon/how-it-works) [[3]](https://www.endura.com/us/en/home.html)

Serious complications are possible, including obstruction, perforation, pancreatitis or unexpected balloon enlargement. Seek urgent medical assessment for severe or persistent abdominal pain, repeated vomiting, inability to keep fluids down or difficulty breathing. [[3]](https://www.endura.com/us/en/home.html) [[5]](https://www.fda.gov/medical-devices/products-and-medical-procedures/weight-loss-and-weight-management-devices)

## Frequently asked questions

### Is a capsule balloon the same as a gastric balloon?

A capsule balloon is one type of gastric balloon. The Allurion system is swallowed, while an endoscopic balloon is placed with a flexible camera passed through the mouth. [[2]](https://www.allurion.com/en-ae/allurion-gastric-balloon/how-it-works) [[3]](https://www.endura.com/us/en/home.html)

### Is a 12-month balloon better than a six-month balloon?

There is no automatic winner. Compare the exact devices, their benefits and risks, the removal schedule and the support programme. Your doctor can explain why a particular duration is appropriate for your circumstances.

### Can I have a gastric balloon during pregnancy?

Weight-loss balloon treatment is not appropriate during pregnancy. Tell your doctor if you are pregnant, breastfeeding or planning pregnancy so they can advise on timing and alternatives. [[3]](https://www.endura.com/us/en/home.html)

### What should I ask about gastric balloon cost in Dubai?

Request a written quote that identifies the balloon model and states whether tests, placement, sedation, medicines, dietitian visits, follow-up and planned removal are included. Ask separately about charges for adjustments, early removal or complication care.
