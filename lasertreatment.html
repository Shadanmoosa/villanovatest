Precision Laser Skin & Body Care

At Royal Villanova Cosmetics & One Day Surgery Center Dubai, we utilize advanced laser technologies to deliver safe and highly effective clinical skin and body care. Under the guidance of our certified laser specialists, we offer customized treatments to address hair reduction, deep skin resurfacing, pigmentation, and tattoo clearance.

Our center is equipped with industry-leading, FDA-approved laser platforms designed to suit all skin types. Whether you are looking for permanent hair clearance with our dual-wavelength laser systems, deep skin remodeling with fractional laser therapy, or clear tattoo removal, we provide a private, comfortable, and luxurious clinical environment.

Permanent Hair Clearance

Laser Hair Removal

State-of-the-art permanent hair reduction using advanced dual-wavelength clinical lasers (featuring Alexandrite and Nd:YAG technologies). Safely targets hair follicles at the root, delivering smooth, long-lasting results for all skin tones and types with integrated cooling systems for maximum comfort.

Featured Device

Pore Purification & Brightening

Carbon Laser Treatment

A highly popular exfoliating and skin-purifying treatment (often called the 'Hollywood Peel'). A layer of liquid carbon is applied to the face, which binds to oil and dead skin cells. The Nd:YAG laser then vaporizes the carbon, instantly cleaning pores, shrinking active acne, and revealing a brighter, refreshed complexion.

Skin Remodeling & Resurfacing

Fractional Laser Treatment

Advanced fractional CO2 laser resurfacing that delivers precise microscopic columns of thermal energy deep into the skin. This triggers a powerful natural healing response and intense collagen remodeling, highly effective for smoothing deep acne scars, tightening wrinkles, and improving overall skin elasticity.

Safe Ink Clearance

Laser Tattoo Removal

Safe and effective clearance of unwanted ink pigments using advanced Q-switched laser technology. The laser pulses break down the tattoo ink particles into tiny fragments, allowing your body's immune system to safely and naturally clear them over a series of sessions, leaving the surrounding skin undamaged.
