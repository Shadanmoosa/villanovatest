BEAUTY THERAPY

Advanced Beauty Therapy & Skin Care

At Royal Villanova Cosmetics & One Day Surgery Center Dubai, our Beauty Therapy department combines high-end clinical treatments with personalized care to restore, protect, and revitalize your skin. Our treatments are tailored to target concerns such as aging, hyperpigmentation, uneven skin tone, acne scarring, and dehydration.

Whether you want to deeply cleanse and hydrate your skin with a HydraFacial, resurface and brighten with a professional chemical peel, or stimulate natural collagen with microneedling, our specialists use the latest techniques to ensure safe, effective, and beautiful results in a premium setting.

Deep Cleansing & Hydration

HydraFacial Deluxe

A premium multi-step skin treatment that combines deep cleansing, exfoliation, extraction, hydration, and antioxidant protection. It uses advanced vortex-fusion technology to deliver nourishing serums deep into the skin, resulting in an instant, long-lasting glow.

Professional Chemical Peels

Collagen Induction

Microneedling (Dermapen)

A minimally invasive skin-rejuvenation procedure using the advanced Dermapen 4 device. It creates controlled micro-channels in the skin to trigger the body's natural healing process, stimulating collagen and elastin production to treat acne scars, pores, and fine lines.

Deep Exfoliation & Pore Care

Carbon Laser Peel

Known as the 'Hollywood Peel', this treatment applies a layer of liquid carbon to the face, which penetrates deep into the pores. The Q-Switched laser energy then targets and vaporizes the carbon, removing dead skin cells, shrinking pores, and reducing active acne.

Cellular Nourishment

Mesotherapy

A customized micro-injection treatment that delivers a blend of vitamins, amino acids, minerals, and hyaluronic acid directly into the mesoderm (middle layer of skin). It deeply hydrates, stimulates collagen, and revitalizes tired or dull-looking skin.

Non-Invasive Body Contouring
