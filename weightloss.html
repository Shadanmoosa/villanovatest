Medical Weight Management & Clinical Nutrition

At Royal Villanova Cosmetics & One Day Surgery Center Dubai, our Weight Loss & Dietetics department bridges advanced medical technology with bespoke nutritional science to help you achieve safe, rapid, and enduring weight loss. We understand that every body is unique, which is why our treatments target metabolic health, hormonal balance, and lifestyle factors.

Whether you are considering a non-surgical gastric balloon for effective portion control, enrolling in our clinical VillaSlim weight loss and body shaping program, or seeking one-on-one dietary consulting with Consultant Clinical Nutritionist Dr. Nayereh Kaboli, our team ensures exceptional care in a luxurious medical setting.

Non-Surgical Endoscopic Weight Loss

Gastric Balloon

A highly effective, non-surgical endoscopic weight loss solution. A soft, medical-grade balloon is placed in the stomach and filled with saline to occupy stomach volume, delaying gastric emptying and curbing appetite. Ideal for patients looking to lose 15kg to 30kg+ safely under dedicated physician and nutritional guidance over 6 to 12 months.

Swallowable Non-Surgical Balloon

Capsule Balloon (Swallowable)

The world’s first swallowable gastric balloon that requires no surgery, no endoscopy, and no anesthesia. In a simple 15-minute outpatient visit, the capsule is swallowed with a glass of water, verified via digital X-ray, and inflated to create lasting fullness. After approximately 16 weeks, the balloon naturally empties and passes comfortably through the digestive tract.
