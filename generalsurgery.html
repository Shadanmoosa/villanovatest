General Surgery Excellence

At Royal Villanova Cosmetics & One Day Surgery Center Dubai, we combine clinical expertise, modern medical technology, and patient-centered care to deliver premium general surgery services. Led by Consultant General Surgeon Dr. Mohammed Abedian, our surgical department specializes in diagnosing and treating common and complex abdominal conditions, focusing on minimally invasive techniques for optimal outcomes.

Our state-of-the-art operating theater and recovery rooms provide a sterile, private, and luxurious environment for your surgical care. We prioritize thorough pre-operative counseling, advanced surgical techniques, and supportive post-operative care to ensure a safe, comfortable, and successful healing journey.

Hemorrhoids Treatment

Relieve pain, swelling, and bleeding with advanced, minimally invasive treatments. We offer customized medical and surgical options, including hemorrhoidectomy, tailored for your complete comfort and recovery.
