DEPARTMENT OF OBSTETRICS & GYNECOLOGY

OBSTETRICS & GYNECOLOGY

Exceptional clinical precision, compassionate care, and personalized women's healthcare in Dubai. Our department provides comprehensive gynecological screenings, advanced pelvic diagnostics, preconception planning, and aesthetic gynecology treatments in total comfort and privacy.

BOOK GYNECOLOGY CONSULTATION

Compassionate Care & Comprehensive Women's Health

At Royal Villanova Cosmetics & One Day Surgery Center Dubai, our Obstetrics & Gynecology Department is committed to providing compassionate, evidence-based medical care tailored to women at every stage of life. From routine preventative screenings to specialized hormonal and reproductive therapies, our priority is your long-term health, comfort, and peace of mind.

With modern high-resolution ultrasound imaging, non-invasive diagnostic protocols, and modern aesthetic gynecology solutions, we ensure that every patient receives individualized, respectful, and confidential medical attention in an elegant, serene clinical environment.

SPECIALIZED EXCELLENCE

Core Clinical Services

Evidence-based medical protocols and specialized diagnostic care designed for comprehensive women’s wellness, vitality, and hormonal health.

Well-Woman & Preventive Care

Annual gynecological examinations, Pap smears, HPV screening, breast examinations, menstrual irregularity evaluation, and routine preventative health guidance.

Advanced Pelvic Ultrasound

High-resolution transvaginal and abdominal ultrasound for uterine and ovarian evaluation, follicular tracking, fibroid assessment, and reproductive diagnosis.

Preconception & Prenatal Care

Pre-pregnancy health assessments, early pregnancy viability scans, antenatal lifestyle guidance, and comprehensive maternal health counseling.

Aesthetic & Regenerative Care

Non-surgical intimate wellness, pelvic muscle rehabilitation, perimenopause and menopause support, and personalized hormonal balance therapies.
