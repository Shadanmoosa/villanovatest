Advanced Men's Health & Specialized Urological Care

At Royal Villanova Cosmetics & One Day Surgery Center Dubai, our Andrology & Urology Department delivers personalized, evidence-based care tailored exclusively to men's reproductive vitality, hormonal balance, and comprehensive urological health. We believe in treating intimate wellness with the utmost discretion, dignity, and clinical excellence.

From innovative therapies for erectile restoration, low testosterone, and Peyronie's disease to advanced fertility diagnostics, prostate health, and urinary tract management, our specialists provide cutting-edge solutions designed to restore confidence, performance, and long-term vitality in an ultra-luxurious private setting.

SPECIALIZED EXCELLENCE

Core Clinical Services

Evidence-based medical protocols and minimally invasive interventions designed for comprehensive male vitality and urinary wellness.

Male Sexual Health & Vitality

Targeted clinical treatments for erectile dysfunction, low-intensity shockwave therapy (Li-SWT), Peyronie’s disease, testosterone replacement therapy (TRT), and premature ejaculation.

Male Fertility & Reproduction

Comprehensive reproductive evaluation, advanced semen analysis, microscopic varicocelectomy, endocrine optimization, and management of male-factor subfertility.

Prostate & Urinary Health

Expert management of benign prostatic hyperplasia (BPH), chronic prostatitis, urinary tract infections, bladder dysfunction, and recurrent kidney stone prevention.

Discreet Diagnostic Suite

Private on-site diagnostics including high-resolution penile Doppler ultrasound, digital uroflowmetry, hormonal profiling, and confidential preventive health screenings.
