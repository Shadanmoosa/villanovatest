SPECIALIZED FACIAL PROCEDURES

Types of Facial Plastic Surgery

Facial plastic surgery focuses on restoring harmony, balance, and youthful definition to the face. Under the expertise of internationally recognized surgeons, our center offers specialized facial procedures including:

Rhinoplasty

Refine the shape, size, and proportions of the nose to enhance overall facial harmony and correct structural breathing concerns.

Blepharoplasty

Refresh tired eyes by removing excess sagging skin, tight muscle tissue, or fat deposits from the upper and lower eyelids.

Facelift

Restore youthfulness to the lower face and neck by lifting sagging skin, smoothing folds, and sharpening the jawline.

Brow Lift

Elevate low or sagging brows that hood the upper eyelids and smooth deep horizontal forehead creases and frown lines.

Chin Contouring

Enhance and strengthen the profile by redefining the chin using specialized implants, custom osteotomy, or targeted fat reduction.

Otoplasty

Reshape protruding, asymmetrical, or large ears to achieve a more balanced appearance closer to the natural head contours.
