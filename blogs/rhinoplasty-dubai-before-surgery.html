Considering Rhinoplasty in Dubai? What to Know Before Surgery

September 2026 6 min read Clinical Review Draft
Clinical assessment: Facial profile examination and surgical planning for cosmetic and structural rhinoplasty at Villanova Cosmetics & One Day Surgery Center Dubai.

Considering rhinoplasty in Dubai often starts with a personal concern: a bump on the bridge, a nasal tip you would like to change, or a nose that feels out of proportion with your face. For some people, difficulty breathing is also part of the conversation.

Before deciding on surgery, it helps to understand what rhinoplasty involves, how surgeons assess suitability, and what recovery can realistically look like. A consultation should give you space to discuss your goals, ask questions and understand the limitations of treatment.

This guide explains the key points to consider before nose surgery in Dubai, from choosing a surgeon to planning time off and reviewing your treatment quotation.

What Is Rhinoplasty?

Rhinoplasty is surgery that changes the shape or structure of the nose. Depending on the individual assessment, it may address appearance, breathing problems related to nasal structure, or both.

Rhinoplasty and septoplasty are different procedures. Septoplasty straightens the septum, the wall separating the nasal passages. Some patients may need both procedures, but a cosmetic change alone does not automatically resolve breathing difficulties. [1]

At your consultation, explain what you would like to change in your own words. For example, “I am concerned about the bump in my side profile” gives the surgeon a clear starting point for discussion.

Rhinoplasty vs Septoplasty: Understanding the Difference

While cosmetic rhinoplasty focuses on altering external cartilages and nasal bones to achieve facial proportion, septoplasty strictly addresses internal airway deviations. When aesthetic reshaping and internal septum correction are combined during a single surgical session, the operation is medically classified as a septorhinoplasty.

Who May Be Suitable for Rhinoplasty?

Potential candidates generally have completed facial growth (typically 16–18 years of age), are physically healthy, do not smoke, and possess realistic expectations about surgical possibilities. The decision should reflect your own wishes, rather than pressure from another person or an unrealistic desire to reproduce someone else's facial appearance. [2]

A consultation is an assessment, not a commitment to surgery. Useful questions to consider beforehand include:

  • What specifically concerns me about my nose?
  • Am I seeking an appearance change, help with breathing, or both?
  • What would a worthwhile improvement mean to me?
  • Can I make time for recovery and follow-up appointments?

Bring these answers with you so the discussion stays focused on your priorities.

What Happens During a Rhinoplasty Consultation?

Your surgeon should discuss your aesthetic goals and review your medical history, allergies, previous operations, regular medications, and supplements. Be completely open about tobacco smoking, vaping, alcohol use, and any previous nasal injury or cosmetic treatment. [2]

The clinical assessment may include examining and measuring your nose, taking standardized multi-angle clinical photographs, discussing appropriate surgical options, and explaining likely outcomes alongside potential risks. Mention breathing difficulties even if your primary motivation for attending is cosmetic refinement.

Photographs or computer simulations may help you and your surgeon discuss possible structural changes. Treat these as anatomical planning aids rather than an absolute promise of your final appearance. Ask how your existing nasal bone structure, cartilage strength, and skin thickness directly affect what can be realistically achieved. [1]

Open vs Closed Rhinoplasty: What Is the Difference?

The clinical terms describe the surgical pathway used by the surgeon to access the underlying cartilage and bone framework of the nose:

Swipe left / right to compare surgical approaches
Approach Where the incisions are made
Open rhinoplasty Incisions are made inside the nostrils, connected by an additional small incision across the columella (the strip of tissue separating the nostrils). Offers maximum direct visibility for complex structural sculpting.
Closed rhinoplasty All incisions are placed entirely within the nasal cavities, without any external columellar incision. Leaves zero visible external scars and typically involves less initial tip swelling.

Depending on the planned structural changes, surgery may involve carefully reshaping bone or cartilage, reducing prominent humps, or adding delicate cartilage grafts for structural support and definition. Graft material commonly comes from the nasal septum or, in secondary or revision cases, from the ear or rib. The anaesthetic plan—whether general anaesthesia or intravenous sedation with local block—depends on the complexity of the operation and clinical assessment. [2]

Ask your surgeon why the proposed approach fits your unique anatomy, where any external incisions will sit, and whether cartilage grafts are expected. The technique name alone does not explain the full treatment plan.

How Should You Prepare for Nose Surgery?

Preoperative preparation may include a comprehensive medical assessment, preoperative laboratory blood tests, and an evaluation of your current medicines. Your surgical team will instruct you on modifying or pausing specific medications or herbal supplements (such as aspirin, ibuprofen, vitamin E, or omega-3 fatty acids) because they can increase bleeding tendencies. Do not stop prescribed treatment without direct guidance from the managing physician. [2]

Smoking cessation is strongly advised well before surgery, as nicotine impairs microvascular blood flow, delays wound healing, and increases the risk of tissue complications. If your procedure is planned as a day surgery, arrange transport home and ensure a responsible adult stays with you for at least the first night.

For clear, practical planning, request written preoperative instructions covering:

  • Fasting guidelines: exact times to stop eating solid food and clear liquids prior to anaesthesia.
  • Which essential daily prescription medications to take on the morning of surgery with a sip of water.
  • Home support requirements and recovery room preparation (cool compresses, extra pillows for elevation).
  • Date and time of your first scheduled postoperative follow-up visit.
  • Emergency clinic contact numbers and on-call medical coordinators for queries outside normal operating hours.

How Much Does Rhinoplasty Cost in Dubai?

Your rhinoplasty quotation must reflect your individual, customized treatment plan. In Dubai, several distinct factors determine the total financial investment:

  • Complexity of surgery: Primary cosmetic reshaping versus revision rhinoplasty, traumatic reconstruction, or combined functional septorhinoplasty.
  • Surgeon's credentials & fee: Board-certified specialist plastic surgeons with dedicated subspecialty mastery in facial aesthetics.
  • Anaesthesia & operating facility: Accredited day surgery hospital facilities, dedicated anaesthetist oversight, and high-tech surgical monitoring.
  • Pre- and post-op care: Diagnostic testing, surgical splints, postoperative medications, and scheduled review consultations.

At Villanova, patients receive an itemized, transparent quotation following an in-person clinical assessment. Before comparing quotes across clinics in the UAE, ensure each proposal covers identical surgical and aftercare inclusions. [2]

Inquire whether your quote covers follow-up visits, splint removal, and whether additional costs apply should revision adjustments or overnight monitoring be clinically indicated. A written breakdown eliminates hidden surprises.

Rhinoplasty Recovery: What Can You Expect?

Recovery progresses in distinct phases. Returning to work and daily social routines occurs on a much earlier timeline than observing the finalized, refined contour of the nasal tip:

Swipe left / right to view recovery milestones
Approximate stage What to plan for
First week An external protective nasal splint remains in place. Periorbital bruising, facial swelling, and a temporary blocked-nose sensation are normal early experiences. Rest with your head elevated on 2–3 pillows.
Around one week Clinical review at Villanova: the surgeon gently removes the external splint and any non-dissolvable columellar sutures. Breathing comfort begins to improve noticeably.
First two weeks Most patients take 10 to 14 days away from work or university. Residual bruising can usually be camouflaged with light mineral makeup once incisions are reviewed.
Following weeks (1–6 weeks) Swelling steadily diminishes. Light walking can resume early; vigorous aerobics, gym workouts, and sports must wait 4 to 6 weeks until cleared by your surgeon.

These are generalized timelines rather than a rigid schedule. Diligently adhere to your personalised discharge guidance, including instructions on head elevation, avoiding vigorous nose blowing, using saline nasal rinses, and protecting the fragile nasal bridge from accidental bumping. [3]

Swelling frequently fluctuates during recovery, often appearing slightly more noticeable in the morning upon waking. If you are travelling to Dubai for medical tourism, discuss your flight arrangements prior to booking; plan to stay in the city for at least 7 to 10 days for splint removal and postoperative monitoring before flying home. [2]

What Are the Risks of Rhinoplasty?

Like all surgical procedures, rhinoplasty carries recognized risks. Potential complications include adverse reactions to anaesthesia, bleeding, infection, altered skin sensation or temporary numbness, persistent breathing resistance, delayed wound healing, visible scarring, and dissatisfaction with aesthetic results. Secondary revision surgery may occasionally be required to address minor asymmetries. [2]

An uncommon complication is septal perforation (a small hole developing in the cartilaginous nasal septum), which can cause crusting or whistling and can be technically challenging to correct.

Ask your plastic surgeon during consultation to clearly explain which risks relate specifically to your anatomical framework and how complications would be handled if they arise. Ensure you have 24/7 clinic contact numbers before departing the surgical facility.

Questions to Ask Before Choosing a Rhinoplasty Surgeon

Use your consultation as an empowering opportunity to understand your surgeon's background, evaluate their clinical approach, and assess the supportive ecosystem available to you:

  • What specific training, board certification, and operative experience do you hold in rhinoplasty?
  • Where will the surgery take place, and what are the day surgery hospital facility credentials?
  • Why do you recommend an open or closed approach specifically for my nasal anatomy?
  • How will you evaluate and safeguard my internal nasal breathing and airway function?
  • What aesthetic outcome is realistic, and what anatomical limitations should I understand?
  • Who manages postoperative care, emergency inquiries, and follow-up reviews?
  • What is your clinical policy and process if a revision procedure is needed?

A reputable plastic surgeon will welcome these questions, fostering transparent alignment on realistic outcomes and patient safety. [2]

Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions patients ask our surgical team during rhinoplasty consultations in Dubai:

Initial improvements become noticeable once the splint is removed after one week and major bruising subsides by week two. However, subtle residual swelling—particularly around the nasal tip—resolves gradually over 9 to 12 months. Final cosmetic and functional results emerge fully around the one-year mark and are generally permanent. [2]
Rhinoplasty can improve breathing when the surgery addresses structural airway blockages, such as a deviated septum or enlarged turbinates. Your surgeon will examine your nasal passages to diagnose the cause; a functional septoplasty or combined septorhinoplasty will be planned accordingly. [1]
Closed rhinoplasty involves incisions exclusively inside the nostrils, leaving zero external visible marks. Open rhinoplasty places an incision across the narrow columellar strip between the nostrils; with careful layered closure, this scar typically matures into an imperceptible, fine line. Discuss your scar management protocol with your surgeon.
Resting eyeglasses or sunglasses directly on the nasal bridge can exert pressure on healing bones and cartilage, potentially displacing newly shaped contours. Most surgeons advise switching to contact lenses or taping glasses to your forehead for 4 to 6 weeks until the bones have fully consolidated. [1]
Most patients feel comfortable returning to desk jobs, remote work, and light social activities after 10 to 14 days, once the nasal splint has been removed and visible bruising has resolved. Jobs involving rigorous physical labor or high contact risks require a longer recovery period of 4 to 6 weeks. [3]
