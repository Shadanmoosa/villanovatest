BOOK ENT CONSULTATION

Advanced Otolaryngology & Micro-Endoscopic Excellence

At Royal Villanova Cosmetics & One Day Surgery Center Dubai, our Ear, Nose and Throat (ENT) department unites surgical precision, ultra-high-definition video endoscopy, and compassionate clinical care. Breathing easily through the nose, hearing with crystal clarity, speaking without vocal fatigue, and sleeping without airway obstruction are foundational pillars of daily vitality and wellness.

Under the direct leadership of Dr. Hamed Abasi (14+ years of specialized surgical experience, fluent in English, Persian, and Arabic), we emphasize meticulous diagnostic investigations—such as high-magnification aural microscopy, chip-on-the-tip flexible rhinolaryngoscopy, and diagnostic audiometry. From non-invasive medical protocols to day-surgery endoscopic procedures, each treatment pathway is engineered for rapid recovery and lasting relief.

FEATURED SPECIALIST

DR. HAMED ABASI

SPECIALIST OTOLARYNGOLOGIST (ENT SURGEON) | 14+ YEARS EXPERIENCE

Dr. Hamed Abasi is a renowned Specialist Otolaryngologist (Head & Neck Surgeon) at Royal Villanova Cosmetics & One Day Surgery Center Dubai. With over 14 years of comprehensive surgical practice, Dr. Abasi brings world-class expertise in endoscopic sinus surgery (FESS), cosmetic and functional septorhinoplasty, microscopic ear surgery, adult and pediatric airway restoration, and snoring disorders.

Fluent in English, Persian, and Arabic, Dr. Abasi is celebrated by his patients for his reassuring bedside manner, exhaustive diagnostic precision, and dedication to tissue-preserving, minimally invasive techniques that ensure swift, pain-free recovery in our luxury private clinical setting.
