DENTISTRY & SMILE DESIGN

Restore your confidence and achieve a flawless, healthy smile with our state-of-the-art dental care and advanced cosmetic dentistry. Under the leadership of board-certified clinical specialists, we offer customized solutions for aesthetic smile makeovers, dental implants, orthodontic solutions, and general dental wellness in Dubai.

Premium Dental Care & Aesthetics

At Royal Villanova Cosmetics & One Day Surgery Center Dubai, we blend advanced clinical precision with cosmetic dental artistry to deliver exceptional results. Under the guidance of our highly experienced dental specialists, we provide custom smile design treatments, restorative dental therapies, and advanced bite corrections designed to optimize your oral health and facial aesthetics.

Our dental department is fully equipped with the latest modern diagnostic technology, digital scanning, and gentle treatment protocols. Whether you are looking to refresh your teeth with professional whitening, choose clear aligners for teeth straightening, or require high-success dental implants, we provide safe, customized solutions in a private, luxurious, and caring environment.

Cosmetic Smile Design

Veneers

Thin, custom-made shells of premium porcelain designed to cover the front surface of your teeth. Perfectly matched to your natural facial geometry, veneers instantly correct discoloration, minor misalignments, chips, and gaps to deliver a flawless, radiant Hollywood smile.

Brighter Smile

Teeth Whitening

Tooth Replacement

Dental Implants

A permanent, highly durable surgical solution for missing teeth. Composed of a medical-grade titanium screw that integrates naturally into the jawbone, completed with a premium ceramic crown that looks, feels, and functions exactly like a natural tooth.

Endodontics

Root Canal Treatment

Advanced endodontic therapy designed to save a severely damaged or infected tooth. The procedure involves carefully removing the infected pulp, sanitizing the root canals under microscopic magnification, and sealing it to eliminate pain and preserve your natural tooth structure.

Restorative Care

Tooth Restoration

State-of-the-art restorative procedures including composite bonding, custom porcelain inlays, onlays, and crowns to restore strength, function, and aesthetics to teeth affected by decay, wear, or trauma.

Orthodontic Braces
